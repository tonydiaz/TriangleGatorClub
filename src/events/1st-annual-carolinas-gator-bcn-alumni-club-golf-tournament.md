The event is presented by Sunbelt Rentals, taking place **March 22–23, 2027**, at the historic Pinehurst Resort & Country Club (https://www.pinehurst.com/). Joining us for an unforgettable experience on Course No. 8 and Thistle Dhu, with all proceeds directly benefiting the M.E. Rinker, Sr. School of Construction Management (https://dcp.ufl.edu/rinker/).



TGC members are invited to play, sponsor, or network with fellow UF alumni and industry leaders in support of the UF Rinker School of Construction Management.



The event has already raised nearly $110,000, and 9 additional team openings at Pinehurst were just unlocked. Spot availability is limited, but payment isn't due until **February 2027**. Check out the attached flyer for full details and reach out soon to secure your team!
