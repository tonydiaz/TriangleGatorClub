Catch the Gators Live in Chapel Hill! 🐊⛳

The UF Women's Golf team is heading to Chapel Hill, NC, to compete in the Tar Heel Invitational at the sceneic UNC Finley Golf Course! Hosted by UNC, this elite collegiate tournament features some of the top golf programs and amateur players in the nation—and admission is completely free for spectators! Come out, support Florida, and watch championship-level golf up close.

* **Dates**: October 16–18, 2026
* **Location**: [UNC Finley Golf Course](https://www.visitchapelhill.org/listing/unc-finley-golf-course/223/) [500 Finley Golf Course Road, Chapel Hill](https://maps.google.com/maps/?daddr=500%20Finley%20Golf%20Course%20RoadChapel%20Hill,%20North%20Carolina)
