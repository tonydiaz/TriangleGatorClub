Catch the Gators Live at The Williams Cup! 🐊⛳

The UF Men’s Golf team is heading to Wilmington, NC, to compete in The Williams Cup at the prestigious Eagle Point Golf Club! Hosted by UNC, this elite 54-hole collegiate tournament features some of the top golf programs and amateur players in the nation—and admission is completely free for spectators! Come out, support Florida, and watch championship-level golf up close.

* **Dates**: October 18–20, 2026

* **Location**: Eagle Point Golf Club (Wilmington, NC)

* **Admission**: Free to attend
